# Client Profile Template

> **This is the single configuration block that powers all 14 skills.** Fill this out once for your company, then copy it into each skill's `## Client Profile` section. When you change companies or clients, swap this block. The methodology stays the same.

---

## How to Use This Template

1. **Fill out every section below** with your company's actual data
2. **Copy the completed profile** into each skill you install (replace the placeholder `## Client Profile` section)
3. **Refine over time** — after running a few skills, you'll spot gaps. Add the missing data.
4. **Keep one master copy** — edit your master, then propagate changes to individual skills

### Quality matters here.

The output of every skill is only as good as the data in your Client Profile. "Reduces costs" as a value proposition produces generic output. "Reduces payment processing time from 12 days to 48 seconds" produces output your reps can actually use in a conversation.

## Quick Validation Checklist

Before you deploy, make sure your profile passes these checks:

- [ ] **ICP is specific** — Could a new BDR read it and correctly qualify 8 out of 10 accounts?
- [ ] **Personas have real language** — Are the pain descriptions from actual call recordings, not your pitch deck?
- [ ] **Proof points have numbers** — Every proof point has at least one specific metric?
- [ ] **Competitive data is current** — Last updated within 90 days?
- [ ] **Value props map to pains** — Every value prop connects to a numbered pain point?
- [ ] **No internal jargon** — Would a prospect understand how you describe their pain?

If you can check all six boxes, your skills will produce high-quality output from day one.
