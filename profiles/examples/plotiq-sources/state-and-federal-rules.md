# State And Federal Rules, Checked Against Primary Sources

Checked on 2026-11-13. These are real rules, not PlotIQ inventions. The PlotIQ profile cites them, so they were checked
against the agencies' own documents before Module 2 uses them. An earlier research note had two of the three wrong.

## Federal: FTC Funeral Rule Penalty

- **Rule:** the maximum civil penalty for a knowing violation of an FTC trade regulation rule, such as the Funeral Rule, is
  **$53,088 per violation**.
- **Source:** 16 CFR 1.98(d), the current eCFR text, which lists "Section 5(m)(1)(A) of the FTC Act, 15 U.S.C.
  45(m)(1)(A): $53,088".
  - eCFR: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-1/section-1.98
  - Set by the January 2025 adjustment: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/17/2025-01361/adjustments-to-civil-penalty-amounts
- **Verdict:** right. The old note had the same figure.
- **Watch:** the FTC adjusts this every January, so check it again each year.

## California: Endowment Care Fund Annual Report

- **Rule:** each cemetery authority files an annual report on its endowment care and special care funds, with a CPA-signed
  audit, "on or before June 1, or within five months after close of the cemetery authority's fiscal year (BPC sections
  7612.6 and 7612.7)".
- **Late filing:** the Bureau may fine up to $400 a month for up to five months.
- **Source:** Cemetery and Funeral Bureau, 2025 annual report form, net income method:
  https://www.cfb.ca.gov/audits/2025ecfscf_report_netincome.pdf
- **Verdict:** **wrong in the old note**, which said May 1. Corrected to June 1, or five months after the fiscal year closes.

## New York: Cemetery Annual Financial Report

- **Rule:** "Every cemetery corporation must file an annual financial report with the division within 90 days following
  the close of its fiscal year." For a calendar-year cemetery, that's usually March 31.
- **CPA reports:** larger cemeteries also file a CPA audit. Cemeteries with $1 million to $10 million in total financial
  assets and under $1 million in annual receipts file a CPA review.
- **Source:** New York Department of State, Division of Cemeteries, Cemetery Operators: https://dos.ny.gov/cemetery-operators
- **Verdict:** **wrong in the old note**, which said a CPA audit is due December 31. Corrected to 90 days after the fiscal
  year closes.
