## The Rules

| Where | What Is Due | When | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| California | Endowment care and special care fund annual report, with a CPA-signed audit | On or before June 1, or within five months after the fiscal year closes. Late fines of up to $400 a month, for up to five months | Cemetery and Funeral Bureau annual report form, BPC section 7612.6 |
| New York | Annual financial report to the Division of Cemeteries. Larger cemeteries add a CPA audit, and mid-sized ones a CPA review | Within 90 days after the fiscal year closes, usually March 31 | New York Department of State, Division of Cemeteries |
| Federal | FTC Funeral Rule. A knowing violation can cost up to $53,088 per violation | Any time, with the amount adjusted each January | 16 CFR 1.98(d) |

## How They Become Urgency

- **Tier A in the Region Map.** States with an annual filing and an audit rank first, because a missed rule costs the most
  there (profile, Outbound Voice). California is Tier A. New York is Tier A for cemeteries large enough to need the CPA
  audit.
- **The trigger is the audit finding.** The window is 60 to 120 days, and the person who acts is the General Manager, or
  the CFO or Controller in a group (profile, Buying Triggers). The deadline in the first line of an email comes from the
  account's own audit letter, never from this table alone.
- **The calendar is a hook.** A California cemetery on a calendar year is assembling its audit between January and June
  1. A New York one is doing it between January and March 31. Discovery Question 2 in the profile ("Walk me through last
  year's trust filing. Who pulled the numbers, and from where?") lands best in those months (illustrative timing).

## What We Do Not Know

- **Rule changes:** the federal figure changes each January, so every row here gets checked again by 2027-01-31.
