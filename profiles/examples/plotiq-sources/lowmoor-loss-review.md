## What The Loss Teaches

1. **"Timing" usually means the board date passed or the champion left** (profile, Loss Taxonomy Mapping). This one was
   the board date.
2. **Decision Process is the deal killer on record** (profile, MEDDPICC). A second killer is `[RECORD]`, because one loss
   can't rank two.
3. **The Discovery to Demo gate asks for the economic buyer by name** (profile, Qualification Stage Gates). At Lowmoor,
   the board date was never confirmed before the proposal went out (illustrative).
