## ICP Definitions

### ICP1: Corporate Legal Departments (Primary)

- **Description:** In-house legal teams at companies with $500M–$5B revenue
- **Key indicator:** 15+ in-house attorneys AND $5M+ annual outside-counsel spend
- **Revenue threshold:** $500M minimum
- **Industry vertical:** Cross-industry (manufacturing, healthcare systems, financial services strongest)
- **Typical titles:** General Counsel, Head of Legal Operations, Deputy GC
- **Why they buy:** Counsel spend is their #1 controllable cost and they can't see it until the invoices land

**Strong Fit Signals:**
- [x] 15+ in-house attorneys
- [x] $5M+ annual outside-counsel spend
- [x] 20+ active outside firms (fragmentation = visibility pain)

**Weak Fit Signals (proceed with caution):**
- [ ] Under 8 attorneys (spend usually too low to justify)
- [ ] Single dominant firm relationship (less fragmentation pain)

## Buyer Personas

| # | Persona Title | Reports To | Top Priority | Key Pain | Hook Focus |
|---|--------------|-----------|-------------|---------|------------|
| 1 | General Counsel | CEO | Legal risk + budget defense | Can't defend spend to the CFO | Audit trail + risk visibility |
| 2 | Head of Legal Operations | GC | Spend + tooling | Invoice review eats the quarter | 20% spend cut, year one |
| 3 | Deputy GC / Managing Counsel | GC | Matter outcomes | No view across 40+ firms | Matter tracking in one place |

**Persona: General Counsel**
- **What they own:** Legal risk, legal budget, board reporting
- **What keeps them up at night:** A budget review where the CFO asks "why did outside counsel cost $8M?" and there's no defensible answer
- **How they measure success:** Spend predictability, zero surprises, risk caught early
- **Objections you'll hear:** "Legal ops tools never stick," "my team has no bandwidth for an implementation"
- **Language they use:** "I can't see it until the invoice lands"

**Persona: Head of Legal Operations**
- **What they own:** Outside-counsel management, e-billing, legal tech stack
- **What keeps them up at night:** Quarter-close invoice review stalls; guidelines exist but aren't enforced
- **How they measure success:** Spend vs. budget by matter, cycle time on invoice review
- **Objections you'll hear:** "We already have e-billing," "switching costs"
- **Language they use:** "We're auditing invoices by hand"

## Core Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | What to Listen For | Business Impact | Frequency |
|---|-----------|-------------------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | No visibility into outside-counsel spend until invoices land | "We find out at quarter close" | 5-12% overbilling goes uncaught | Nearly every deal |
| 2 | Manual invoice review | "Someone reads every line item" / "we spot-check" | 3 attorney-weeks per quarter | Very common |
| 3 | Matter status scattered across 40+ firms | "We email the firm for updates" | 3-week delays on live litigation decisions | Common at Enterprise |
| 4 | Billing-guideline violations unenforced | "Guidelines exist but..." | Rate creep, block billing, staffing violations | Common |
| 5 | No data for firm negotiations | "We renew at whatever they propose" | Leverage lost at every rate negotiation | Enterprise deals |
